Most modern local networks support mDNS. Teachers can type your host computer's hostname in their browsers instead of an IP address. This friendly hostname remains permanent even if your router assigns the machine a brand-new IP.

☢️ Full System Factory Reset

This action completely deletes the passport_tracker.db file and initializes a fresh database configuration. All configurations, logs, users, grades, and rosters will be permanently lost.
